## 🧠 About These Notes

Each note is structured as a full study session covering:

- **Overview** — A plain-English introduction to the topic
- **Sections** — Bite-sized breakdowns with bullet points and real-world examples
- **Key Points** — One essential takeaway per section
- **Summary** — The 5 most important facts to remember
- **R.I.C.E / frameworks** — Where applicable, structured methods and tables

Notes are written at a **beginner level** with clear language, analogies, and no unnecessary jargon — perfect for revision or first-time learning.
